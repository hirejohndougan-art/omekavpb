VISUAL PAGE BUILDER for Omeka S
USER GUIDE: Simple mode (for volunteers)
========================================================================

This guide is for people who add and check items in the Omeka admin.
Simple mode is the default. It shows plain words and only the screens
you need. Your site manager can switch on Expert mode if you need the
Omeka screens; see "Simple and Expert" at the end.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE SIDEBAR
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Home         The starting page: recent work and the search report.
Items        Find and open items in the collection.
Add an item  Add something new, step by step (see below).
Files        Pictures and documents attached to items.
Item sets    Groups of items.
Import       Bring in items from a spreadsheet (when it is set up).
Settings     Search words that mean the same thing (see "Search words").
People       Who can sign in and what they can do.
Questions    The labels and help text on the Add an item form.
Glossary     Plain explanations of the words used in this admin.

The page you are on is shown in bold in the sidebar.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADDING AN ITEM
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Choose "Add an item" in the sidebar. There are three steps. You can
leave at any point; nothing is public until you publish it.

Step 1: Files
  Choose one or more pictures or documents from your computer and
  press "Add files and continue". You can skip this step with "Skip for
  now" and add files later from the item's Files tab.

Step 2: Describe
  Fill in the title (required), then any of: description, creator,
  date, subject and place. Each question has a short note underneath.
  The preview on the side shows how the item will look as you type.
  Press "Save and continue".

Step 3: Where it shows
  Tick each site the item should appear on. Then choose one:
    Publish         The item is public on the ticked sites.
    Keep as draft   The item is saved but not public (staff only).
    Save only       The item is saved as it is now. Nothing is made
                    public by this button.

Publish needs a title. When you finish, the item opens on its own screen.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
FINDING AND CHANGING AN ITEM
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Items shows the collection. Type words in the search box to narrow it.
Press an item's title to open it. The item screen has four tabs:

  Details        The title, description and other facts. Change them and
                 press "Save details". A blank title is not saved, so the
                 old title stays.
  Where it shows The sites the item shows on, and the Public tick.
                 Press "Save where it shows".
  Files          The pictures and documents. Add a file, remove one, or
                 press "Make main image" on the one you want.
  History        Earlier versions of the item. Press "Restore this
                 version" to go back to one. Each change keeps the
                 version it replaces.

Actions at the bottom of the item screen:
  Duplicate  Makes a copy of the item. The copy is a draft, staff only.
  Delete     Removes the item. Tick "I understand: deleting cannot be
             undone from here" first, then press "Delete this item".


------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE QUESTIONS LABELS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The labels and help text you see on the Add an item form come from
Questions. Your site manager can change the wording there. Title is
always required.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SEARCH WORDS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

On the Settings page, under Search synonyms, each line says words that
mean the same thing, written as:

    rr = railroad
    station = depot

Someone who searches for "rr" will also find items that say "railroad".
The rule works one way only: searching "railroad" does not find "rr"
unless you also add a line for it. Only the lines with an equals sign
are used. Only a global administrator can change Settings.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE SEARCH REPORT
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Home shows the words people searched for on the public site in the
last 30 days: the most common ones, and the ones that found nothing.
If a word keeps finding nothing, consider adding it as a search word
or adding the missing item.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
SIMPLE AND EXPERT
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Expert mode adds the Omeka screens to the sidebar, shows the Omeka ID
of each item, and shows the Dublin Core code (for example dcterms:title)
beside each question. It
is switched on from the bottom of the sidebar, separately for each
person. Switch back to Simple at any time.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
GETTING HELP
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Open Glossary in the sidebar for the meaning of words such as draft,
public and main picture. For anything else, ask the site manager.
