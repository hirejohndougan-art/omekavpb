VISUAL PAGE BUILDER for Omeka S
with the Companion Theme for Visual Page Builder
========================================================================

This download has three things in it:

  VisualPageBuilder                   The module (the page editor, Special
                                      Blocks, page templates, menu builder).
                                      Goes in Omeka's "modules" folder.

  visual-page-builder-companion       The Companion Theme (how your public
                                      site looks). Goes in Omeka's "themes"
                                      folder.

  HOW TO INSTALL AND USE.txt          This file.

License: GNU General Public License, version 2 or later (GPL-2.0-or-later).
See the LICENSE file in each folder.

You need Omeka S 4.1 or later. To check, look at the bottom of any page in
your Omeka admin; it shows the version.

The module works with any theme. The Companion Theme is optional, but it is
the only theme that shows the menu styles from the menu builder, and it is
made to show pages exactly the way you build them.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Back up your Omeka database. (Ask your web host if you're not sure how.)
   Every install or upgrade of any module should start with a backup.

2. Try it on a test copy of your site first if you have one.

3. You'll need a way to upload folders to your web server, such as your
   host's file manager or an FTP program (FileZilla, WinSCP).

4. Do NOT open the folders and upload only what's inside them. Upload each
   folder as a whole, keeping its exact name.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1. INSTALL THE MODULE
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Upload the folder "VisualPageBuilder" into the "modules" folder of your
   Omeka installation. When you're done, this file must exist:

       modules/VisualPageBuilder/Module.php

   If a "VisualPageBuilder" folder is already there (an older version),
   delete it first, then upload the new one. Deleting the folder does not
   delete your pages; they are stored in the database.

2. In your Omeka admin, click "Modules" in the left menu.

3. Find "Visual Page Builder" and click "Install". If you had an older
   version, the button says "Upgrade" instead; click it.

Upgrading from the old "Society Blocks" module? First, under Modules,
click "Uninstall" next to Society Blocks (your pages keep their blocks),
then delete the "SocietyBlocks" folder from "modules", then do the steps
above. The upgrade moves those blocks over with their settings.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2. INSTALL THE COMPANION THEME (optional, recommended)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Upload the folder "visual-page-builder-companion" into the "themes"
   folder of your Omeka installation. When you're done, this file must
   exist:

       themes/visual-page-builder-companion/config/theme.ini

   (If you have a folder named "page-builder" in "themes" from an earlier
   test, you can delete it. That was the same theme under its old name.)

2. In your Omeka admin, go to Sites, open your site, and click "Theme".

3. Choose "Companion Theme for Visual Page Builder" and save.

4. Still under Theme, open the theme's settings (the gear or "Edit theme
   settings") to set:
     - your logo (upload it under Assets first),
     - accent, header, and background colors,
     - heading and text fonts,
     - page width,
     - a search box in the header,
     - footer text and Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube links,
     - whether the footer shows your visit info (address and hours).


------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 3. USING IT
------------------------------------------------------------------------

EDITING A PAGE VISUALLY
  Go to Modules > Visual Builder (with more than one site, choose yours).
  One page shows everything for the site: Edit header, Edit footer, Edit
  menu, the theme, and the list of pages. Click "Open visual editor" next
  to a page ("Find a page" helps on big sites). Or go to
  Sites > your site > Pages, open a page, and click "Open visual editor" at
  the top. The page opens full screen.

  - Sections hold columns, and columns hold blocks.
  - Add things from the sidebar on the left: drag them onto the page, or
    click them to add them after whatever is selected.
  - Click anything on the page to change its settings in the sidebar.
  - Drag the bar between two columns to change their widths.
  - A bar with a + in a box shows above, between, and below sections. Drag
    a section or block onto it, or click the + and then pick a section or
    block in the sidebar; it goes right there. Escape cancels.
  - Type text straight onto the page.
  - Every block in the sidebar has an (i) button that explains what it does.
  - Undo and Redo are the curved arrows at the top (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y).
  - "Preview" hides the editing marks. "Save" saves (or press Ctrl+S).
  - "Preview on site" shows the unsaved page on your real site, with your
    theme, header, and footer, at desktop, tablet, or phone width. Nothing
    is published; visitors can't see it.
  - Text formatting is in the sidebar when a text block is selected:
    Style (paragraph, heading 2-4, quote), Font (10 web-safe fonts), Size,
    Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough, text color, highlight color,
    Clear formatting, align left / center / right / justify, bulleted and
    numbered lists, quote, link, remove link, and footnote. Select words
    on the page, then click; the buttons light up to show what's applied.
    To add a picture to text, put the cursor there and click "Insert an
    image" in the sidebar.
  - Every Special Block has a Title (change it) and "Show the title" (turn
    it off to hide it) at the top of its settings. Its Background color
    is further down, under Style.
  - Section settings > Smaller screens: on tablets, one per row, two per
    row, or as on desktop; on phones, one or two per row. The Tablet and
    Phone buttons at the top show the result.
  - While editing a page, your site's header shows above it and the footer
    below (faded; that's how the Companion Theme shows them). Click either
    to edit it. If the page has unsaved changes, you're asked first: Save
    and continue, Continue without saving, or Cancel. While editing the
    header or footer, a band for the page leads back to the page you came
    from, the same way.
  - "<- Visual Builder" at the top left takes you back to the site's page
    under Modules (it warns you first if there are unsaved changes).
  - Drag the sidebar's right edge to make it wider.
  - Backgrounds: select a section, column, or block and pick one of 18
    colors under Background, or any color with the custom color picker
    (works the same for pages, headers, and footers). On dark colors the
    text turns white by itself.
  - Background images: under Background image, choose a picture from
    Assets, then Fill the area / Whole picture, Top / Center / Bottom, and
    "Darken it so text is readable".

  The sidebar has three tabs:
  - Blocks: everything you can add, with a "Find a block" search.
  - Layers: an outline of the page; click a row to select it.
  - Templates: 30 ready-made pages (home, browse, story, about, contact,
    events and support). "Use for this page" replaces the page (it asks
    first); "Add to the end" keeps what's there. Replace the [bracketed]
    text with your own. "Ready-made sections" offers 10 single sections
    (text beside a photo, call to action band, quote band, and more) to add
    anywhere, including where you clicked a +.

SPECIAL BLOCKS
  The "Special Blocks" group in the sidebar has 47 extra blocks: random and
  recent items, item of the day, on this day, collection cards and counts,
  search box, hero banner, then-and-now photo slider, quotes, FAQ
  sections, buttons, visit info, upcoming events, announcement bar,
  slideshow, gallery, explore-by-topic tiles, browse by decade, subjects,
  oral history with transcript, document with transcription, people,
  partners, fundraising progress, newsletter sign-up, how to cite, and for
  headers and footers: logo, site title, site menu, social links, and a
  copyright line. Also: who's who in a photo (numbered markers on people),
  item table, milestones timeline, did you know?, open now (from your
  weekly hours), embeds from trusted sites (Google Maps and Forms, YouTube,
  Vimeo, Internet Archive), share this page, and breadcrumbs.
  New in 1.1: a Map of places (OpenStreetMap; click the map in the sidebar
  to drop a pin, then name it and link an item; or turn it into a numbered walking
  tour) and a Timeline (items in date order plus your own entries).
  Timeline entries and map places each have a title, a date (timeline) or
  spot (map), a linked item from the archive, a picture (the item's, one
  you choose, or none), and details. In the sidebar each entry shows a
  one-line summary; click it to open and edit it.
  New in 1.6: the Story map. A map with a timeline under it: visitors press
  Play and the map moves from stop to stop in date order while a pane tells
  each stop's story (background photo, text, YouTube/Vimeo or the item's
  own video, link to the item). Back to the start, Previous, Next, and the
  timeline stops work by hand. Click the map in the sidebar to add a stop,
  then give it a name and a date.
  New in 1.7: the Archive quiz. Multiple-choice questions whose answers
  visitors find by searching the archive. Each question can have a ready-
  made "Search the archive" link, a hint, and, after answering, an
  explanation and a link to the item where the answer is. Score at the end,
  "Try again" to start over. Answers are checked in the visitor's browser
  and nothing is stored; it's for fun, not a test.
  New in 1.8: the Oral history block syncs when the transcript has times.
  Type times at the start of lines, like [00:12:40] or 12:40, or attach a
  caption file (.vtt or .srt) to the item and choose "A caption file on
  the item". Then the line being spoken is highlighted and followed, any
  line or time can be clicked to jump there, visitors can search inside
  the interview, video gets captions, and an optional Topic index lists
  where each subject starts. Note: Omeka doesn't accept .vtt or .srt files
  by default; add "vtt, srt" to the allowed file extensions and "text/vtt,
  text/plain, application/x-subrip" to the allowed media types in Omeka's
  Settings > Security first.
  New in 1.14.1: change the table of contents from its own settings. Under
  "Found on this page", each heading and Table link has arrows to indent
  (make it a subsection) or outdent it, and a grip you can drag sideways
  (or use the left/right arrow keys on). Indenting a heading changes its
  style on the page, Heading 2 -> 3 -> 4, like a word processor. Click an
  entry to rename it in the table of contents, leave it out, or pick its
  level from a list.
  New in 1.14: the Table of contents builds itself from your headings,
  like a word processor. In a text block, set a line's Style to Heading 2
  (a section), Heading 3 (a subsection), or Heading 4 (a sub-subsection),
  and the "Table of contents (this page)" block lists it, nested, in page
  order (optionally numbered 1, 1.1, 2...). Click into a heading and, in
  its Text formatting panel under Table of contents, untick "Show in the
  table of contents" to leave it out, or give it a shorter "Name in the
  table of contents". For a spot with no heading (a map, a gallery), drag
  in a Table link block and name it: on the site it takes no space; in the
  editor it's a thin dashed bar you can drag. The Table of contents'
  settings list everything it found on the page. Every heading also gets
  its own link (page address + #the-heading-words). The old "Link name"
  setting is retired: spots that already have one keep it (shown as
  "Older link name" in their settings) and keep working.
  New in 1.7: the Archive quiz. Multiple-choice questions whose answers
  visitors find by searching the archive. Each question can have a ready-
  made "Search the archive" link, a hint, and, after answering, an
  explanation and a link to the item where the answer is. Score at the end,
  "Try again" to start over. Answers are checked in the visitor's browser
  and nothing is stored; it's for fun, not a test.
  New in 1.8: the Oral history block syncs when the transcript has times.
  Type times at the start of lines, like [00:12:40] or 12:40, or attach a
  caption file (.vtt or .srt) to the item and choose "A caption file on
  the item". Then the line being spoken is highlighted and followed, any
  line or time can be clicked to jump there, visitors can search inside
  the interview, video gets captions, and an optional Topic index lists
  where each subject starts. Note: Omeka doesn't accept .vtt or .srt files
  by default; add "vtt, srt" to the allowed file extensions and "text/vtt,
  text/plain, application/x-subrip" to the allowed media types in Omeka's
  Settings > Security first.
  New in 1.9: link names and Jump links. Give any section or block a Link
  name (its settings, under Style), like visit-us, and link straight to
  that spot: your page address + #visit-us ("Copy link" copies it). A
  "Name in Jump links" (the table of contents) sets how it's listed. Headings you type get link
  names automatically. The Table of contents (jump links) block is an "On this page" list that
  fills itself in with ONLY the spots you've named (or, if you choose,
  your headings too), in page order. When making a link in text, the
  prompt suggests the spots on the page.
  New in 1.7: the Archive quiz. Multiple-choice questions whose answers are
  found by searching the archive: each question can have a "Search the
  archive" link with suggested words, a hint, the item where the answer is,
  and an explanation shown after answering. Visitors check each answer and
  see their score at the end. Answers are checked in the visitor's browser
  and nothing is stored; it's for fun, not a graded test.

  Blocks that show items let you choose where the items come from: this
  site, sites you pick, or all sites.

VISIT INFO (enter it once)
  Sites > your site > Settings has a "Visit info" section for hours,
  address, admission, and contact details. Any Visit info block set to
  "A site's visit info" shows it, and so does the Companion Theme's footer.
  Hours go one row per line, like:  Tuesday to Saturday | 10 am to 4 pm
  Closed dates go one per line, like:  2026-11-26 | Thanksgiving
  "Open now" blocks read both, so they say whether you're open right now.

WHO'S WHO IN THIS PHOTO
  Choose the photo, then click on it in the sidebar to drop a numbered
  marker on each person; drag a marker to move it. Type each name in the
  Markers list.

EVENTS FROM YOUR CALENDAR
  An Upcoming events block can show events from Google Calendar or similar.
  Paste the calendar's public iCal address (ends in .ics) into the block.
  In Google Calendar: Settings > your calendar > "Public address in iCal
  format".

HEADER AND FOOTER
  Modules > Visual Builder > Edit header / Edit footer (or the "Header"
  and "Footer" buttons in the page editor) opens your site's header or
  footer in the same visual editor as pages.
  - Start from a template: the Templates tab offers five headers (logo and
    menu, centered, two rows with search, with an announcement bar,
    minimal) and five footers (simple, three columns, newsletter and
    social, partners strip, large with site map).
  - Or build your own: the sidebar shows only header and footer elements
    there (Logo, Site title, Site menu, Search box, Social links, Copyright
    line, Breadcrumbs, Buttons, Visit info, Open now, and a few more).
  - Save, and every page of the site shows it.
  Built headers and footers show with the Companion Theme. Until you build
  one, the theme uses its own simple header and footer. Other themes keep
  their own header and footer.

THE THEME BUILDER (new in 2.0)
  Modules > Visual Builder > your site > Theme Builder.
  - Templates: one design for one kind of screen (Item, Media, Item set,
    Browse items, Search results, Page not found, Header, Footer). The most
    specific template wins: one record, then its resource template, item
    set, class, and finally all. A template goes live only when its
    required blocks are in place. (Public screens use live templates once
    the item screens are connected, in a later release.)
  - Global styles: the primary, secondary, text, background and link colors,
    text size, and corners. They apply to every page and template on the site.
  - A site that upgrades keeps its look: the new settings start at the
    defaults, which match 1.x.

THE MENU BUILDER
  Modules > Visual Builder > Edit menu (or the "Menu" button in the page
  editor).
  - Add pages, web addresses, "Browse items", and "Collections".
  - Use the arrows to reorder; "Indent" puts an item under the one above.
  - Pick one of 19 menu styles; each shows a small picture of your menu
    in that style. New in 1.16: five vertical styles that run down the side
    of the page (side menu left or right, a list with everything showing,
    sub-pages that fly out, links in a panel), and Colors: pick a scheme or
    set your own menu background, link, highlight, and drop-down colors.
  - Desktop / Tablet / Phone at the top switch the preview, which shows
    your site with your changes before you save (with the Companion Theme).
  - In a header you build, the Site menu block always uses this page's
    style and colors.
  - The original 15: five have your logo
    centered with the menu underneath (plain, in a color band, newspaper
    masthead, with dividers, small capitals); others include drop-downs,
    large panels, side menu, logo in the middle, logo left with menu right,
    rounded buttons, tabs, two rows, and a single Menu button.
  - Click Save.
  The menu itself works with any theme. The style only shows with the
  Companion Theme.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
GOOD TO KNOW
------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Your pages stay normal Omeka pages. If you ever remove the module,
  regular blocks keep showing (one under another instead of in columns).
  Special Blocks stop showing until the module is put back; their settings
  are kept.

- Keep Omeka's "Use HTMLPurifier" setting on (Settings > Security). It
  cleans the formatted text in some Special Blocks.

- "Today" and "now" (announcements, events, open now, and so on) follow the
  time zone in Omeka's Settings > General. Make sure it's yours.

- Some themes have special "page templates" (like a Homepage template).
  Pages saved in the visual editor always use the standard one, so your
  columns show as you built them.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The module or theme doesn't appear in Omeka:
  The folder is probably in the wrong place or has an extra folder inside
  it. Check that these exact files exist:
      modules/VisualPageBuilder/Module.php
      themes/visual-page-builder-companion/config/theme.ini

"This module requires a newer version of Omeka":
  Upgrade Omeka S to 4.1 or later.

No "Open visual editor" button on a page:
  Check that the module shows as installed and active under Modules, and
  that your user account is allowed to edit that site's pages.

Upcoming events don't show calendar events:
  The calendar address must be public, and your web server must be able to
  reach it. Events are checked at most once an hour.

Anything else: take a screenshot of what you see and note what you
clicked just before.

SPECIAL BLOCKS IN GROUPS (new in 1.13)
  In the sidebar's Blocks tab, the Special Blocks are sorted into groups:
  From your collection, Telling history, Maps, Recordings and documents,
  Visiting and events, Getting involved, Finding your way, and Reuse.
  Click a group's heading to fold it (remembered); searching opens every
  group with a match. Each block's (i) info says what it works well with.

PAGE TOOLS (new in 1.12)
  The "Page" button at the top of the editor:
  - History: a copy of the page is kept every time you save in the visual
    editor (the newest 50). Load one to bring it back as unsaved changes;
    Undo takes it back, Save keeps it.
  - Download layout / Import layout: save the page's layout as a file (a
    backup, or to use on another Omeka site), and load one back, replacing
    the page or adding to the end. Items and pictures from another site
    need choosing again.
  - Check page: lists common problems (pictures without descriptions,
    skipped heading levels, links that go nowhere or to a missing spot,
    "click here" links, empty sections, blocks missing what they need),
    each with "Show me". It never blocks saving.
  On the Visual Builder page:
  - Duplicate (next to each page) makes a private copy and opens it.
  - Shared sections: build a section once and show it on many pages with
    the "Shared section" block; change it once and every page updates.
    Make one from scratch here, or from any section in the editor
    (section settings > "Make this a shared section").
  Footnotes: while typing, the footnote button (superscript 1) adds a note
  where the cursor is. On the page notes are numbered and listed under
  "Notes" at the end, or wherever you put a Footnotes block.
  Printing: printed pages drop menus, search boxes, buttons and map
  controls, print link addresses after links, and avoid splitting blocks.
